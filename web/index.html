      Hand-checked from WHO, PAHO, UNEP, CDC and peer-reviewed sources. Red = waterborne outbreak, amber = river contamination.
      Method
      Keys: / search · E T R modes · G globe · 3 3D · L hide panel · Esc close. Layers, basemap and open sections are remembered in this browser.
      On the map: 2.37 M river reaches ≥ 1 m³/s, 1,131 river systems with a main stem, 185,000 lakes ≥ 1 km² checked by every trace, 11 historic events.
      River network: HydroRIVERS v1.0 – every reach knows the reach it flows into. A trace follows that chain to the sea or an inland sink.
      Tested out of sample on Haiti 2010 (cholera from the UN camp stream at Meille down the Artibonite): path, order of arrival and timing pass; the speed of spread across the delta and the 5 km exposure proxy fail – canals carry river water beyond the band and across the delta within a day. So the Risk tab ranks places by people on unsafe water and shows ranges, never counts of the ill or the dead. Details: docs/VALIDATION.md.
      Travel time per reach = length ÷ v, with v = 0.10 · Q0.30 m/s (Q = mean discharge). Calibrated on 6 reported arrival times (spills at Baia Mare 2000, Songhua 2005, Rio Doce 2015 and the Rhine water transit Rheinfelden–Lobith): typical out-of-sample error ×1.17. Lakes are listed, not added. Mean flow only – not a forecast.
